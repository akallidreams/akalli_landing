# **App Name**: Akalli Agents

## Core Features:

- Balder Agent Highlight: Showcase Balder, the AI customer support agent with its capabilities (text, audio, image recognition, and audio response).
- Demo Generator: Employ AI to generate a short demonstration of Balder in action. Display examples of customer interactions and successful automated sales.
- Agent Placeholder: Include placeholders (greyed out) for the 7 upcoming AI agents, creating anticipation and a sense of future expansion.
- Mission Statement: Design a clear and concise explanation of Akalli's mission and vision in creating AI agents that revolutionize customer support and sales.
- Contact/Demo Request: Incorporate a contact form or call-to-action button, encouraging potential clients to request a demo or further information about Akalli's AI agents.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A sophisticated blue (#4682B4) to convey trust and innovation, reflecting the advanced AI technology. It evokes intelligence and reliability.
- Background color: Light desaturated blue (#D1DDE6) for a clean and professional look. It keeps the focus on the content and conveys trustworthiness.
- Accent color: A contrasting warm orange (#FF8C00) to highlight call-to-action buttons and important information, drawing the user's eye to key interaction points. This also conveys energy and a solution-oriented approach.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', a geometric sans-serif for a modern, fashionable, and precise look. For the body text use 'Inter' which is a grotesque-style sans-serif, lending a contemporary feel to longer content.
- Use modern, minimalist icons to represent agent capabilities and features.
- Implement a clean, single-page layout with clear sections for each agent and feature, promoting ease of navigation and engagement.
- Incorporate subtle animations and transitions to enhance the user experience and create a sense of technological advancement.
